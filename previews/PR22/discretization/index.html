Discretization

FEMDiscretization is exported, as are the PDEBase names a model needs (MeshDomain, boundarypiece, boundarypieces, NormalDerivative) and Ferrite's Lagrange and reference shapes. Getting started shows them in use.

FiniteElementMethod.FEMDiscretization — Type
FEMDiscretization(Ω::MeshDomain, t = nothing; spaces = Dict(), quadrature = nothing,
    test_space = Galerkin(), ibp = Dict(), pairing = Dict(), codegen = :numeric,
    dirichlet = :algebraic, kwargs...)

Finite-element discretization of a PDESystem whose spatial domain is the mesh domain Ω (of a Ferrite.Grid), with time variable t. DAEProblem(pdesys, disc) assembles the Galerkin operators with Ferrite and returns a DAEProblem with one array unknown per field.

  • spaces: dependent variable (u or u(t, x, y)) => Ferrite.Interpolation; the default is Lagrange{RefShape, 1}() on the mesh's reference shape. All fields must currently share one interpolation.
  • quadrature: quadrature order; nothing means 2p for Lagrange order p.
  • ibp: equation (or its index) => PDEBase.StrongForm integration-by-parts policy.
  • pairing: equation => dependent variable it is tested with, needed for an equation with no time derivative of its own, such as 0 ~ Dxx(v) + f.
  • codegen = :mtk and dirichlet = :eliminate are reserved for later milestones and raise; remaining keywords are passed to the DAEProblem, where a keyword given at the DAEProblem/discretize call wins. iv, unknowns, du0, coordinate and name are set by the discretization and rejected.

Pieces come from boundarypiece.(Ω, names); on an analytic DomainSets box write boundarypiece.(Ref(rect), names), since DomainSets types do not broadcast as scalars.

Where pieces with Dirichlet conditions on the same field meet (a corner), the condition listed last in the PDESystem's boundary conditions wins, as in Ferrite's ConstraintHandler. Listing the same piece and field twice is an error. Fields are numbered in one order throughout (DofHandler blocks, array unknowns, FEMMetadata.fields), the declared order of the PDESystem's dependent variables (PDEBase.StrongForm.declared_fields); equations are paired with fields through PDEBase.StrongForm.pair_equations.

Parameters are substituted by the values given in the PDESystem when the operators are assembled, so remake with new parameter values does not reassemble them.

SciMLBase.DAEProblem — Method
DAEProblem(pdesys::PDESystem, disc::FEMDiscretization; kwargs...)

Discretize pdesys with disc and return the DAEProblem M du + K u = F(t) of the Galerkin operators, with Dirichlet rows u_d = g_d(t) as algebraic equations, a sparse analytic Jacobian and the FEMMetadata as its problem_type, so solve returns a PDETimeSeriesSolution. Nothing calls mtkcompile; anything this milestone cannot discretize raises. Keywords are passed to the DAEProblem after disc.kwargs, so a keyword given here wins; the keywords the discretization sets (iv, unknowns, du0, coordinate, name) are rejected.

The Dirichlet rows are linear with identity Jacobian rows, so a Newton step with a current Jacobian satisfies them exactly. OrdinaryDiffEq's BDF methods reuse the Jacobian across step-size changes and then rescale the Newton step, which leaves these rows off by about the solver tolerance; DFBDF(nlsolve = NLNewton(new_W_dt_cutoff = 0)) refreshes it on every step size change and holds u(t, Γ) ~ g(t) to round-off.

FiniteElementMethod.Galerkin — Type
Galerkin()

Bubnov–Galerkin testing: every equation is tested with the space of its paired field. The default, and the only test space before SUPG lands.

Restrictions of this release:

  • Geometries are Ferrite.Grid meshes (1D, 2D and 3D cell shapes) wrapped as MeshDomain(grid), with boundary pieces named by the grid's facet sets. Only 1D and 2D are evidenced; meshes with mixed cell types are rejected until M4.
  • Coordinates are Cartesian and named by the (x, y) ∈ Ω pairing. Time must be the first argument of every dependent variable.
  • Dirichlet data may depend on space, time and parameters but not on another field. Neumann and Robin conditions are written with NormalDerivative(Γ), with any conormal coefficient written explicitly. Neumann and Robin data may depend on time; the Robin coefficient multiplying u(t, Γ) may depend only on space and parameters until M3.
  • Periodic conditions u(t, Γa) ~ u(t, Γb) are rejected until M4. Interface conditions between regions have no syntax; every field lives on the whole mesh.
  • Boundary pieces must be disjoint. A piece with no condition gets the natural zero-flux condition, with a warning when the operator has a flux.
  • Where two Dirichlet pieces of one field meet, the condition listed last in the PDESystem wins.
  • Every field with a time derivative needs an initial condition, linear in that one field and finite at every node.
  • Steady problems, dirichlet = :eliminate, fields with different interpolations and test spaces other than Galerkin() are rejected until M4. Nonlinear operators, time-dependent mass or stiffness coefficients and codegen = :mtk are rejected until M3.
