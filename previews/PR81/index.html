FiniteElementMethod.jl

FiniteElementMethod.jl is a SciML discretizer that takes a ModelingToolkit.jl PDESystem in strong form on a mesh, derives the Galerkin weak form, assembles it with Ferrite.jl and lowers it to a DAEProblem.

Warning

This package is under construction. Problems with Galerkin or SUPG testing are supported, including nonlinear terms whose state dependence is in the coefficients, sources and natural conditions (∇·((1 + u²)∇u), u ∂ₓu, u², ∂ₙu = −u⁴); periodic conditions and time-dependent mass or stiffness coefficients raise an error. Boundary conditions and limitations lists every restriction.

Getting started solves a heat equation on a Gmsh mesh. A MethodOfLines user should then read Differences from MethodOfLines: coordinates are bound differently, boundary conditions are written on named boundary pieces, and solutions have different shapes. Supported equation classes states what is supported and on what evidence.
