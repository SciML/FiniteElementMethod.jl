(a) Accuracy per degree of freedom: convdiff2 against MethodOfLines

max nodal error at t = 30 against the steady solution; FEM P1 Galerkin vs MethodOfLines (default upwind advection), same nodes. FEM solver: DFBDF(nlsolve = NLNewton(newWdt_cutoff = 0)) on the DAEProblem from FEMDiscretization; reltol = 1.0e-9, abstol = 1.0e-11. MethodOfLines solver: DFBDF() on the DAEProblem from MethodOfLines; reltol = 1.0e-9, abstol = 1.0e-11. The FEM column is Galerkin P1, which is centred and second order; MethodOfLines' default upwinds the advection term, which is first order. On these meshes convdiff2 has a cell Péclet number below one (Tier 1 of the supported-classes table); advection-dominated problems stay Tier 2 until SUPG.
